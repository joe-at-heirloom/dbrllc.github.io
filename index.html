Common questions

Things people ask.

What size projects do you take?

Most of my work runs from a single room — a kitchen refresh, a stair rebuild, a closet system — to a full ground-floor remodel. Smallest jobs tend to be a built-in or a mantel. Largest are kitchen-and-bath combinations that take a couple of months. If you're not sure whether a project is a fit, ask.

Do you handle design as well as the build?

Yes. For most projects I'll sketch the work and walk you through materials before quoting. For larger renovations where you're working with an architect or interior designer, I'll build to their drawings. Either way you get one carpenter on site.

How long does a kitchen take?

A refacing or partial refresh is usually 2–4 weeks. A full kitchen remodel — cabinets out, plumbing and electrical coordinated, tile, trim, finish — runs 6–10 weeks depending on scope and material lead times. I'll give you a real timeline with the quote, not a placeholder.

What about permits and inspections?

I pull permits when the work requires them — most kitchen remodels, anything structural, any plumbing or electrical changes. If a job doesn't need a permit, I'll tell you that too. I work with electricians and plumbers I've used for years for the parts of a project that need licensed trades.

How does payment work?

A deposit at the start, a progress payment when materials arrive on site, and the balance at completion. Larger projects get a milestone schedule written into the quote. No surprise charges — if scope changes mid-project, we agree on the change in writing before I do the work.

Where do you work?

Grosse Pointe (all five communities), the Lake St. Clair lakeshore, and St. Clair Shores. Macomb, Clinton Township, Chesterfield, and select Metro Detroit projects on request. If you're outside that zone, ask anyway.
