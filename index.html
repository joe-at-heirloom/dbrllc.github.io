About

Ian O'Ray.

I'm a carpenter. I started Design Build & Renovate so I could do every part of a project myself — measure it, draw it, build it, install it. No middlemen. The work is the work.

Most of what I do lives in the in-between: a kitchen that needs more than a refresh but less than a gut, a staircase that's been waiting twenty years, a closet that should have been built right the first time. Good work, fair prices.
